# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want Ghost Macro Recorder to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Good behaviour includes:

- being kind and respectful, and assuming good intent
- giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- sexualised language or imagery
- publishing other people's private information without permission
- any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainer, [@mohnishkrishna-saikumar-mk9](https://github.com/mohnishkrishna-saikumar-mk9),
by opening an issue or through GitHub. Reports are handled privately and fairly. The maintainer may remove,
edit or reject comments, commits or other contributions that break this code, and may ban contributors who
repeatedly do so.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
